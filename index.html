This week in AI

OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra, a frontier model, and GPT-6.2 Sol, a cheaper everyday model, across ChatGPT Work, Codex and the API. It also launched ChatGPT for Sales, with a Sales plugin and more than 30 partner plugins. Google made Tasks in Gemini free in six countries and put Gemini 4 Hydrogen into Search worldwide.

Astra and Sol is the story clients will ask about, because OpenAI pitches better results with fewer tokens. Test that on your own tasks rather than trusting the launch claims. The Sales plugins and Astra for Law matter if you advise sales or legal teams, and Enterprise customers can request Law access.

Claude added whiteboard, sheet and data features, covered in two short videos and a longer walkthrough below. Claude Code shipped 2.1.326 and 2.1.327 with small safety and clarity fixes. To try something this afternoon, last30days-skill gathers research on a topic and book-to-skill turns a PDF into a skill. Read the thread behind the 40 percent search traffic claim before you repeat it to a client.
