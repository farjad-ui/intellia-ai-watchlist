This week in AI

There were no big model launches this week; the news is about standards and rollouts. Anthropic donated the Agent Skills format to the Agentic AI Foundation, with more than 70 platforms already supporting it, and OpenAI released a Decisions API for fast, low-cost calls.

For a consultant, the practical point is that skills are becoming portable. A skill written for Claude is more likely to work in tools your clients already use, so building a small library of them is a safer bet than it was a month ago.

Two enterprise stories show what adoption looks like. Uber is rolling ChatGPT Work out to more than 30,000 staff, and a restaurant franchisee cut cash reconciliation time by 94%. Use them as reference points for large and small clients.

Price is the other client question. A widely discussed post looks at why OpenAI and Anthropic cut prices by up to 80% within months, and IBM's CEO argues open-weight models are now cheap and good enough to run on-premises.

To try this week: Hallmark for less generic design, Claude SEO for marketing audits, and VoiceStudio for local transcription and dubbing. Claude Code 2.1.309 also fixes sandbox gaps, which matters for security-minded clients.
