This week in AI

Google announced Gemini 4 Neon and opened Genie 4 to users, and Microsoft showed seven new models at Ignite. OpenAI added ChatGPT for Accounting and ChatGPT Wallet, which lets agents buy things with your approval. Claude Code 2.1.328 brought auto mode to Claude on Bedrock, Google Cloud and Foundry.

Gemini 4 Neon is the story clients are most likely to raise, since early commentary already compares it with Claude. Read the announcement and one hands-on opinion before you form a view. ChatGPT for Accounting matters if you advise finance teams that use QuickBooks, and the Claude Code change matters for clients who reach Claude through a cloud provider.

To try something this afternoon, n8n builds workflow automations visually, and ffmpeg-skill edits video on your own machine. Anthropic also priced its initial public offering this week, which clients may mention in passing. The KPMG audit trial report is worth a read before your next finance conversation.
