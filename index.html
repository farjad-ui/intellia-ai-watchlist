This week in AI

OpenAI launched GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol on Tuesday, then spent the week on ChatGPT plugins for sales and data work, including a Data agent that builds dashboards and shared reports. Claude Code 2.1.329 added warnings about where hooks come from, and Google put Tool Retrieval in preview in the Gemini API.

For a consultant, the Data plugin videos are the best five minutes of the week: they show a team going from a spreadsheet to a shared dashboard without an engineer. Clients on ChatGPT may ask about the sales and healthcare plugins, and legal clients about Astra for Law.

Anthropic's Nasdaq debut is the big story clients are likely to mention, and Reuters has the overview. To try something this afternoon, MarkItDown turns office documents into text for AI tools, and diagram-design makes clean process diagrams in Claude Code.
