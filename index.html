This week in AI

The one change that affects planning is OpenAI lowering usage limits on GPT-6.2. An OpenAI engineer says Astra now hits its cap 40% sooner and Sol 25% sooner. Claude Code and the Gemini API had smaller updates, covered below.

For a consultant, the useful clips this week are OpenAI's walkthroughs of Finances in ChatGPT and of a dot that watches projects while you are away. Both show agent-style work in a form a client can follow in a few minutes.

On the tools side, last30days and diagram-design are the easiest to try, though both need a Claude Code setup. Markitdown helps when you need to feed client documents to an assistant. Clients may also ask about the Photoshop warning from the New York Times.
